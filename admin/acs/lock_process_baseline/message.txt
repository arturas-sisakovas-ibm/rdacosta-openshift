========
Purpose:
========
To demonstrate how ACS learns which binaries typically run inside a container and how you can lock this "process baseline" to detect and block anything that deviates — effectively creating an allowlist of binaries per workload.

====================
Why should I listen?
====================
This is the closest thing to AppArmor or SELinux policies in Kubernetes-native form. It empowers platform teams to lock down known-good behaviour per workload, drastically limiting the blast radius of zero-days or container escapes.

========
Prereqs:
========

Completion of enable_runtime_detection

ACS Sensor installed and healthy

BusyBox or any test pod running with a known startup behaviour
