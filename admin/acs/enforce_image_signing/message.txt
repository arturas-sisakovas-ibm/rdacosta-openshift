========
Purpose:
========
To demonstrate how ACS can require all container images to be signed using a trusted signature source (e.g. Cosign, Red Hat signatures), and block unsigned or tampered images from being deployed.

====================
Why should I listen?
====================
Unsigned images can:

Be modified by attackers after scanning

Be pulled from unauthorised or untrusted sources

Introduce supply chain risks

This demo helps students understand the software supply chain attack surface — and how ACS acts as a gatekeeper to prevent it.

========
Prereqs:
========

ACS Sensor and Central installed

OpenShift cluster with internet access

cosign installed on your workstation (optional, for signature creation)

A signed image from a known source (or simulate one)

Namespace: sig-demo
